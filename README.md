This was my final project for CS 1083 (Intro to Comp Prog II in Java). 

Don't forget to press "Confirm Selection", "Submit Email", and "Place Order". 
Your email must include an '@' symbol to be considered valid. 
Note that it takes a couple seconds to make your drink before you can collect it once you place your order. 
If you don't enter a name for a new order, your order will go to the person who ordered before you!
You can also create a file with all orders that have been made in that session. 
Your points are saved to your email during a session!
